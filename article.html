Building a Premium Modular Portfolio with Custom SVG Graphics

July 9, 2026 • 5 min read

Creating a unique identity on the web requires more than just templates. Modern premium websites, like those designed by Vercel, Apple, and Linear, utilize a blend of luxury typography, tight vertical rhythm, and interactive lighting elements to create a high-value aesthetic.

The Role of SVG Lighting

Background radial gradients and blurred elements form a key pillar of contemporary digital design. Rather than loading massive background images, using inline SVGs with standard Gaussian blur filters lets us render smooth, scalable gradients that adapt to any screen size seamlessly.
